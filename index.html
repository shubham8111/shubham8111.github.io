Long Drive

A calm, endless drive through real places, running in the browser. No score, no timer, nothing to win. It is meant to be left on like a fireplace video.

Roads, terrain, buildings and imagery come from open map data, so Bryce Canyon, Manhattan, Paris, Los Angeles, London and three Grand Prix circuits sit where they really are. There is a day and night cycle, weather, and a radio.
